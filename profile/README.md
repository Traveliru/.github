Traveliru gives hotels, B&Bs and vacation rentals their own tour page — with their name and logo, ready in less than 30 minutes, and free. Guests browse, book and pay in a couple of taps; the host earns a commission on every tour booked.

Seven in ten travellers choose their tours before they arrive *(Arival + Phocuswright, 2023)*. Without a tour page of your own, they book somewhere else.

## How it works — three steps, only one of them is yours

1. **We build your page. You share the link** — in your pre-check-in messages, on your website, or a QR code at the property.
2. **The best local tours are already on it** — you pick which ones show, and can change them at any time.
3. **Guests book and pay online. We handle it.** Confirmations, changes, cancellations and refunds — and payouts to you and the tour operators.

Free for hosts: no subscriptions, no setup fees, no monthly cost. We earn when a guest books a tour — and so do you.

**Tour operators:** your tours appear on the tour pages of hotels and Airbnbs near you, reaching guests you couldn't reach before. Guests pay online; we pay you.
